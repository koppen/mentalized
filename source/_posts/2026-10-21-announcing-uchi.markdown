I have used pretty much every admin panel in existence. I’ve even been content with some, but mostly it has always felt like settling. Sure, in the beginning it’s great, but then there’s that point where you need to do something custom and everything breaks down.

<!--more-->

## Here be dragons

So you end up hacking your way around limitations, but down that path is nothing but a mess that have to wade through every time you upgrade.

Or you figure you might as well build the admin area yourself; “It’s just a bit of CRUD, how hard can to be?”. Been there, done that, and it inevitably leads to a bunch of ugly, almost usable pages where you have to know exactly what buttons to touch and which melt everything down.

In my experience, admin sections end up a neglected, desolate place, where only the initiared dare thread. That’s a shame.

## Your admins deserve better

Your admins, your support staff, your employees, all deserve a friendly, usable, and pretty interface to have their back as they perform their daily tasks.

That’s why we’ve built [Uchi](https://www.uchiadmin.com). [A premium admin panel framework](https://www.uchiadmin.com) that doesn’t just give you a delicious CRUD UI, but also lets you adapt and extend without getting in your way. There's no custom DSLs or complex configuration files, because it’s just Rails.
